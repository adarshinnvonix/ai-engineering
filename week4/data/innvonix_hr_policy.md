# Innvonix Technologies — Employee HR Policy Handbook

## 1. Leave Policy

Innvonix provides employees with the following leave categories each calendar year:

- **Casual Leave (CL):** Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per year, credited at
  1 per month. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and must be applied
  for at least 1 day in advance, except in emergencies.
- **Sick Leave (SL):** Employees receive 8 sick leaves per year. A medical certificate is
  required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- **Earned/Annual Leave (EL):** Employees accrue 1.5 earned leave days per month (18 per
  year) after completing 6 months of service. Earned leave can be carried forward up to a
  maximum of 30 days; anything beyond that is lapsed at year-end unless encashed.
- **Maternity Leave:** Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave as
  per the Maternity Benefit Act, applicable for the first two children.
- **Paternity Leave:** Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave,
  to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.
- **Bereavement Leave:** 5 days of paid leave in the event of the death of an immediate family
  member.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance for
planned leave. Unplanned/emergency leave must be informed to the reporting manager on the same
day, with formal application filed within 48 hours.

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance

Standard working hours at Innvonix are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday, with a
1-hour lunch break. Core hours (mandatory presence, whether in-office or online) are 11:00 AM
to 4:00 PM. Employees are expected to complete 8 productive hours per day.

Attendance is tracked through the biometric system (for office staff) or the online attendance
portal (for remote staff). Three or more instances of unexplained late arrival (after 10:00 AM)
in a month will result in a formal warning from HR.

## 3. Remote Work / Work From Home Policy

Innvonix supports a hybrid work model. Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week
with prior manager approval. Fully remote arrangements are considered on a case-by-case basis
and require approval from both the reporting manager and HR, typically for roles that do not
require regular on-site presence or for employees relocating for personal reasons.

Employees working remotely must:
- Be reachable during core hours (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM).
- Have a stable internet connection and attend all scheduled video meetings with camera on.
- Log their work status on the internal collaboration tool at the start and end of each day.

Remote work privileges may be revoked if attendance, availability, or output standards are not
maintained.

## 4. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to maintain professionalism, integrity, and respect in the
workplace. The following are considered serious violations of the Code of Conduct and may
result in disciplinary action up to and including termination:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind (see Anti-Harassment Policy below).
- Sharing confidential company or client information with unauthorized parties.
- Misuse of company assets (laptops, software licenses, credentials) for personal gain.
- Conflicts of interest not disclosed to management (e.g. moonlighting for a competitor).
- Falsification of timesheets, expense claims, or official records.

Minor violations (e.g. dress code non-compliance, repeated tardiness) will typically result in
a verbal warning followed by a written warning before escalation.

## 5. Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees who incur business-related expenses (travel, client meals, approved software
subscriptions, etc.) can claim reimbursement through the Expense Management Portal.

- All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred, along with valid
  receipts/invoices.
- Local travel (cab/auto for client visits) is reimbursed at actual cost with receipts.
- Outstation travel requires pre-approval from the reporting manager before booking, and is
  reimbursed as per the travel grade applicable to the employee's band.
- Claims without valid receipts above ₹500 will not be processed, except for petty cash items
  under ₹500 which may be self-certified.
- Reimbursements are processed within 15 working days of approval, along with the monthly
  payroll cycle.

## 6. Employee Benefits

Innvonix provides the following benefits to all full-time employees:

- **Health Insurance:** Group medical insurance covering the employee, spouse, and up to 2
  children, with a sum insured of ₹5,00,000 per family per year. Pre-existing conditions are
  covered from day one of joining.
- **Accident Insurance:** Personal accident cover of ₹10,00,000 for all employees.
- **Provident Fund (PF):** As per statutory requirements, both employer and employee contribute
  12% of basic salary to PF.
- **Wellness Allowance:** ₹5,000 per year for gym memberships, mental health counseling
  sessions, or wellness apps, reimbursable via the Expense Management Portal.
- **Learning & Development:** Up to ₹20,000 per year for approved certifications, courses, or
  conference attendance relevant to the employee's role.

## 7. Termination and Notice Period

Either the employee or Innvonix may terminate employment by providing notice as per the
employee's contract:

- **Probationary employees** (first 3 months): 15 days' notice period.
- **Confirmed employees below Manager level:** 30 days' notice period.
- **Manager level and above:** 60 days' notice period.

Innvonix may, at its discretion, offer payment in lieu of notice (either partially or fully) to
either party. Full and final settlement, including pending salary, leave encashment, and
reimbursements, will be processed within 45 days of the last working day.

Immediate termination without notice may occur in cases of proven gross misconduct, as
determined by HR and management following an internal inquiry.

## 8. Anti-Harassment Policy

Innvonix maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward workplace harassment, including sexual
harassment, bullying, and discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, disability, or
sexual orientation.

Employees who experience or witness harassment are encouraged to report it to the Internal
Complaints Committee (ICC) via email at icc@innvonix.example or through the confidential HR
hotline. All complaints are investigated within 90 days as per the POSH Act, 2013, with strict
confidentiality maintained for all parties involved. Retaliation against a complainant is
itself treated as a serious Code of Conduct violation.
